About this blog

Every property we seriously consider gets a page: the listing price and photos, what we'd do to it, what that would cost, what it would actually be worth — and the verdict. No vibes-based purchasing. If the maths doesn't work, the property gets the stamp, and the stamp is not kind.

Posts are written from public listing data, town bylaws, contractor pricing and actual sold comps, checked on the date shown. Estimates are planning numbers, not quotes or appraisals. Listing photos belong to their MLS/brokerage; we save and credit them per listing.
