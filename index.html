About this blog

Every house we seriously consider gets a post: what it is, what we'd do to it, what that would cost, what it would actually be worth afterwards — and the verdict. No vibes-based purchasing. If the maths doesn't work, the house gets the stamp, and the stamp is not kind.

Posts are written from public listing data, town bylaws, contractor pricing and actual sold comps, checked on the date shown. Estimates are planning numbers, not quotes or appraisals.
