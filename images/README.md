# Listing photos

One photo per listing lives here, named after its post (e.g. `3-balsam-dr.jpg`),
and is shown on the listing's card on the home page:

```html
<div class="listing-photo"><img src="images/3-balsam-dr.jpg" alt="3 Balsam Dr, Chelmsford"></div>
```

Photos come from the MLS/listing Parthey sends. They remain the property of
their MLS/brokerage — this folder is for the photos of properties under
genuine investigation on this search journal, credited on the property's page.
If a photo can't be saved, the card keeps its placeholder slot.
